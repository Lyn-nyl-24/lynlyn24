    G000024Valuative (v) topologyTopology

    Valuative refinement · Structure · The common qcqs ambient category specified in the valuative diagram.

    For a map of qcqs schemes Y→XY\to X, test every Spec⁡V→X\operatorname{Spec}V\to X for a valuation ring VV. There must be an injective local map of valuation rings V↪WV\hookrightarrow W and a lift Spec⁡W→Y\operatorname{Spec}W\to Y over XX. For families, work affine locally on the target and take finite affine refinements whose disjoint union passes the test.

    G000024Valuative (v) topologyTopology

    Valuative refinement · The common qcqs ambient category specified in the valuative diagram.

    For a map of qcqs schemes Y→XY\to X, test every Spec⁡V→X\operatorname{Spec}V\to X for a valuation ring VV. There must be an injective local map of valuation rings V↪WV\hookrightarrow W and a lift Spec⁡W→Y\operatorname{Spec}W\to Y over XX. For families, work affine locally on the target and take finite affine refinements whose disjoint union passes the test.

    Bhatt–Mathew, The arc-topology · Stacks 0ETA · valuation lifting and comparison with fpqc and ph · Stacks 0EVM · affine inverse limits of h and ph covers

    Topological spaces

    The spectrum of a discrete valuation ring has one generic point and one closed point. It is spectral, but the generic point is not closed. In the patch topology both singletons are open, so that topology is finite discrete.

    X000014The p-adic integers are profinite but not extremally disconnectedExample

    Topological spaces

    The p-adic integers are the inverse limit of the finite discrete quotient rings modulo powers of p. The subset of nonzero elements with even p-adic valuation is open. Its closure adds 0, which is not interior because every neighbourhood of 0 also contains elements of odd valuation.

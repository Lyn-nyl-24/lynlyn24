Relations between properties of morphisms

Implications and equivalences for a morphism of schemes, based on Görtz and Wedhorn’s Appendix D.

DiagramEquivalencesAll relations

The source calls universally injective morphisms purely inseparable. Radicial means universally injective. The labels ft, fp and lfp mean finite type, finite presentation and locally of finite presentation. The labels qc and qcqs mean quasi-compact and quasi-compact quasi-separated, respectively. Dashed arrows require the indicated hypothesis. Heavier arrows are the implications singled out in Appendix D. Select a node or arrow to read its Stacks references. Use Full size to pan the map, or Relations to browse it as text.

Görtz and Wedhorn, Algebraic Geometry I: Schemes, 2nd ed., Appendix D, pp. 585–586

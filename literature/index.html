Spectral sequence literature

Begin with filtrations and exact couples, then choose applications or convergence.

On Lynlyn24: a computed filtered complex and an unrolled exact couple.

First reading and algebraic constructions

  • Spectral sequences: filtered complexes

    The Stacks Project · Homological Algebra, §12.24, Tag 012K · Open reference

    Start here for decreasing filtrations, bidegrees, and the relation between the limiting page and the filtration on cohomology.

  • Spectral sequences: double complexes

    The Stacks Project · Homological Algebra, §12.25, Tag 012X · Open reference

    Compare the two filtrations of a total complex and see how the order of taking cohomology changes the early pages.

  • An Introduction to Homological Algebra

    Charles A. Weibel · Cambridge University Press, 1994, Chapter 5, pp. 120–159 · Publisher

    Read alongside the filtered-complex example for exact couples, convergence and spectral sequences of derived functors.

Topology and worked applications

  • Spectral Sequences

    Allen Hatcher · Algebraic Topology, supplementary Chapter 5, §§5.1–5.2 · Author’s PDF

    Begin with the exact-couple discussion and the Serre spectral sequence. Continue to §5.2 for the Adams construction.

  • A User’s Guide to Spectral Sequences

    John McCleary · 2nd edition, Cambridge University Press · Publisher

    Use Part I for the algebraic foundations and Part II to compare Serre, Eilenberg–Moore, Adams and Bockstein examples.

  • The Adams spectral sequence for the image-of-J spectrum

    Robert R. Bruner and John Rognes · arXiv:2105.02601 · Open preprint

    An advanced example relating an Adams differential to Yoneda composition in the long exact sequence of a cofiber sequence.

Convergence and unbounded filtrations

  • Conditionally Convergent Spectral Sequences

    J. Michael Boardman · Contemporary Mathematics 239 (1999), pp. 49–84 · Publisher

    Consult §§5–8 when bounded convergence theorems do not apply, especially for entering and exiting differentials or whole-plane examples.

Definition 8.37 · Irreducible components#

(i) The irreducible components of a topological space TT are the maximal irreducible subsets of T.T.

(ii) The irreducible components of a scheme XX are the irreducible components of the underlying space ∣X∣.\vert X\vert .

Remark 8.38#

Let TT be a topological space.

(i) By Zorn’s lemma, every irreducible subset of TT is contained in an irreducible component of T.T. In particular, since points are irreducible, TT is the union of its irreducible components.

(ii) A subset A⊂TA\subset T is irreducible if and only if its closure is. It follows that the irreducible components of TT are closed.

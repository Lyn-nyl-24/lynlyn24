Consider a monic polynomial f∈k[x]f\in k[x] over a field k.k. By the functorial Nullstellensatz (Corollary 2.25), we know that ff is determined by its zero sets in all kk-algebras. Example 2.94 · Geometry in mixed characteristic#

Another aspect that is not captured by classical algebraic geometry over fields is algebraic geometry in mixed characteristic, i.e., involving rings RR that do not contain any field. This is especially relevant in number theory, which studies rings of integers in finite extensions of ℚ.\mathbb{Q}. Such rings can map to fields with different characteristics, which sometimes allows us to transport results from one characteristic to another.

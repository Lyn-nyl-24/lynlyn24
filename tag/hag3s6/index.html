Definition 3.44 · Loci associated with a linear map#

Let AA be a ring and let f:M→Nf:M\rightarrow N be a map of AA-modules. We define the following subfunctors of Spec(A):A):

∙\bullet The vanishing locus V(f)f) of ff is the locus where ff is zero; ∙\bullet The epimorphism locus Epi(f)f) of ff is the locus where ff is surjective; ∙\bullet The monomorphism locus Mono(f)f) of ff is the locus where ff is universally injective; ∙\bullet The isomorphism locus Iso(f)f) of ff is the locus where ff is bijective.

If NN is a line (resp. if MM is a line), the epimorphism locus (resp. the monomorphism locus) of ff is also called the nonvanishing locus of ff and denoted by D(f).f).

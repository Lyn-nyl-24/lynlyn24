Warning 4.26#

Different closed subfunctors can have the same open complement, since different ideals can have the same radical (e.g., (x)(x) and (x2)(x^{2}) in ℤ[x]).\mathbb{Z}[x]). For this reason, there is no notion of “closed complement” of an open subfunctor.

The loci from Definition 3.44 have a direct generalization to any algebraic functor X:X: a map f:M→Nf:M\to N in Mod defines subfunctors V(f),f), Epi(f),f), Mono(f),f), and Iso(f)f) of X.X.

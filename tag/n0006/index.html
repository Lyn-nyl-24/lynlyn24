A spectral sequence with a nonzero d₂

Compute the pages of a filtered complex over F₂, including a differential that requires a corrected lift.

  • A finite filtered complex

A finite filtered complex #

Use a decreasing filtration on a cochain complex over 𝔽2\mathbb{F}_2. A generator of filtration pp belongs to FsCnF^sC^n exactly when s≤ps\leq p. The differential dd has total degree one and preserves the filtration.

Generator Total degree nn Filtration pp dd
aa 0 0 bb
xx 1 0 yy
bb 1 1 00
uu 1 1 00
zz 1 1 y+wy+w
yy 2 1 00
ww 2 2 00
vv 2 3 00

Computing the pages #

Write n=p+qn=p+q. For r≥1r\geq1, use the subspaces

Zrp,n={c∈FpCn:dc∈Fp+rCn+1}Erp,q=Zrp,nZr−1p+1,n+dZr−1p−r+1,n−1\begin{aligned} Z_r^{p,n}&=\{c\in F^pC^n:dc\in F^{p+r}C^{n+1}\}\\ E_r^{p,q}&=\frac{Z_r^{p,n}}{Z_{r-1}^{p+1,n}+dZ_{r-1}^{p-r+1,n-1}} \end{aligned}

Here Z0p,n=FpCnZ_0^{p,n}=F^pC^n. The map drd_r is induced by dd and has bidegree (r,1−r)(r,1-r). The diagrams below are generated by binary row reduction on this input. The basis table on each page gives the chosen lifts.

Each label gives the vector space Erp,qE_r^{p,q} up to isomorphism. Blank positions are zero. Arrows show nonzero drd_r.

  • On E0E_0, the filtration-preserving part sends zz to yy, so those classes vanish on E1E_1.
  • On E1E_1, d1[a]=[b]d_1[a]=[b]. The class of xx survives because y=d0zy=d_0z on the associated graded.
  • On E2E_2, lift the surviving class by x+zx+z, since d(x+z)=wd(x+z)=w. Therefore d2[x+z]=[w]d_2[x+z]=[w].
  • On E3=E∞E_3=E_\infty, the survivors are [u][u] in bidegree (1,0)(1,0) and [v][v] in bidegree (3,−1)(3,-1). Directly, H1(C)H^1(C) is generated by the class of uu and H2(C)H^2(C) by the class of vv, with these filtration degrees.

The calculation applies to finite filtered complexes over 𝔽2\mathbb{F}_2 given by an explicit filtered basis and differential. General coefficient rings, multiplicative extensions, and infinite filtrations require separate input and arguments.

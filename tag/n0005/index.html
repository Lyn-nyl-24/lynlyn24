A morphism of exact sequences #

Work in the category of abelian groups. The rows below are short exact, and the squares commute.

Theorem 3.1.1 · Snake lemma for short exact rows#

There is an exact sequence

0⟶ker⁡f′⟶ker⁡f⟶ker⁡f′′→δcoker⁡f′⟶coker⁡f⟶coker⁡f′′⟶00\longrightarrow\ker f'\longrightarrow\ker f\longrightarrow\ker f'' \xrightarrow{\delta}\operatorname{coker}f' \longrightarrow\operatorname{coker}f\longrightarrow\operatorname{coker}f'' \longrightarrow 0

Proof · Snake lemma for short exact rows#

For x∈ker⁡f′′x\in\ker f'', choose a∈Aa\in A with pA(a)=xp_A(a)=x. Commutativity gives pB(f(a))=0p_B(f(a))=0, so there is a unique b′∈B′b'\in B' with iB(b′)=f(a)i_B(b')=f(a). Define δ(x)=[b′]\delta(x)=[b']. A different lift of xx differs by iA(a′)i_A(a') and changes b′b' by f′(a′)f'(a'). This proves well-definedness, and taking sums of lifts proves additivity.

If δ(x)=0\delta(x)=0, write b′=f′(a′)b'=f'(a'). Then a−iA(a′)a-i_A(a') is in ker⁡f\ker f and still maps to xx. Conversely, lifting xx by an element of ker⁡f\ker f gives δ(x)=0\delta(x)=0. This proves exactness at ker⁡f′′\ker f''.

A class [b′][b'] maps to zero in coker⁡f\operatorname{coker}f exactly when iB(b′)=f(a)i_B(b')=f(a) for some aa. In that case pA(a)∈ker⁡f′′p_A(a)\in\ker f'' and δ(pA(a))=[b′]\delta(p_A(a))=[b']. For exactness at coker⁡f\operatorname{coker}f, if pB(b)=f′′(a′′)p_B(b)=f''(a''), lift a′′a'' to aa. Then b−f(a)b-f(a) is in the image of iBi_B. Exactness at ker⁡f\ker f follows by lifting along iAi_A, and the two endpoint assertions follow from injectivity of iAi_A and surjectivity of pBp_B.

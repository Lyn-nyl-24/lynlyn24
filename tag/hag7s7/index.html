7.7 · Tag 0056

Fpqc descent

Section 7.7 · Schemes

The fpqc topology on CAlg was defined in Example 6.29. Although schemes are by definition Zariski sheaves on CAlg it turns out that they are in fact fpqc sheaves. Moreover, almost all of the Zariski descent results established so far remain true if we replace the Zariski topology by the fpqc topology. Such results are very powerful, because the fpqc topology is much finer than the Zariski topology. For example, any field extension is an fpqc covering, so we can often reduce questions over general fields to the case of algebraically closed fields, where there are more tools at our disposal (e.g., the Nullstellensatz).

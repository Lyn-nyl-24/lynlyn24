Conventions and notation

  • Throughout, “ring” means “commutative ring” and “algebra” means “commutative algebra”. Not necessarily commutative algebras will be called “associative algebras”.
  • We denote by CAlg the category of (commutative) rings. Given R∈R\in CAlg, we denote by CAlg the category of (commutative) RR-algebras.
  • Given a ring RR and a subset S⊂R,S\subset R, we denote by R[S−1]R[S^{- 1}] the localization of RR at S.S. When S={f}S=\{f\} has single element, we also write RfR_{f} or for the localization.

Remark 4.16 · Pushforward of quasi-coherent modules#

Let f:Y→Xf:Y\rightarrow X be a map of algebraic functors. For quasi-coherent modules and algebras, the pushforward functor f∗f_{\ast} always exists, modulo “size issues” as in Remark 2.53. That is, if MM is a quasi-coherent module over Y,Y, then f∗(M)f_{\ast}(M) is potentially a large quasi-coherent module over X.X. This does not happen if XX and YY are accessible, hence does not happen in practice.

Warning 4.19#

Beware that the forgetful functors Rad Id Mod are not compatible with base change, and so do not extend to arbitrary algebraic functors XX (although they will when XX is a scheme, see §7.6): a quasi-coherent radical ideal over XX need not have an underlying quasi-coherent ideal, and a quasi-coherent ideal over XX need not have an underlying quasi-coherent module.

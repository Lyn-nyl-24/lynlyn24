Affine, projective, and general schemes

Section 1.5 · Some examples

In §1.1 and §1.2 we saw examples of affine schemes, and in §1.3 and §1.4 we saw examples of projective schemes. The former are the solutions in affine space of systems of polynomial equations, while the latter are the solutions in projective space of systems of homogeneous polynomial equations. If we allow inequations in addition to equations, we obtain the notions of quasi-affine and quasi-projective schemes. We will study affine schemes in §2 and projective schemes in §3. All of these objects are examples of schemes, which we will finally define in §7. The following diagram summarizes the situation:

Warning 10.67#

Let XX be a scheme.

(i) By definition, 𝒦X\mathcal{K}_{X} is the sheafification of a presheaf whose value on any affine open U⊂XU\subset X is the total ring of fractions Frac(𝒪(U)).\mathcal{O}(U)).

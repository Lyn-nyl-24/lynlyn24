Warning 10.51 · Relation to regular schemes#

By Remark 10.50, a noetherian local ring RR is regular if and only if its maximal ideal 𝔪⊂R\mathfrak{m}\subset R is regular. Suppose that XX is a regular scheme and that is a closed immersion.

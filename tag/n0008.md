## Number systems {#sec:number-notation}

The standard number systems are the natural numbers \(\mathbb{N}\), the integers \(\mathbb{Z}\), the rational numbers \(\mathbb{Q}\), the real numbers \(\mathbb{R}\), and the complex numbers \(\mathbb{C}\). When relevant, specify whether \(\mathbb{N}\) includes zero. Write \(\mathbb{F}_q\) for the finite field of order \(q\), where \(q\) is a prime power.

## Projective varieties {#sec:projective-notation}

Write \(\mathbb{P}^n_k\) for projective \(n\)-space over the field \(k\). A projective variety \(X\) may be presented by a closed immersion \(X\hookrightarrow\mathbb{P}^n_k\). The double-struck \(\mathbb{P}\) names the ambient projective space; the symbol \(X\) denotes the chosen variety.

## Sphere spectrum {#sec:sphere-notation}

Write \(\mathbb{S}=\Sigma^\infty S^0\) for the sphere spectrum, where \(S^0\) is the pointed zero-sphere. The notation \(S^n\) denotes a sphere as a space, while \(\pi_n(\mathbb{S})\) is a stable homotopy group.

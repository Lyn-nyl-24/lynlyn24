## A finite filtered complex {#sec:filtered-example}

Use a decreasing filtration on a cochain complex over $\mathbb{F}_2$. A generator of filtration $p$ belongs to $F^sC^n$ exactly when $s\leq p$. The differential \(d\) has total degree one and preserves the filtration.

| Generator | Total degree \(n\) | Filtration \(p\) | \(d\) |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| \(a\) | 0 | 0 | \(b\) |
| \(x\) | 1 | 0 | \(y\) |
| \(b\) | 1 | 1 | \(0\) |
| \(u\) | 1 | 1 | \(0\) |
| \(z\) | 1 | 1 | \(y+w\) |
| \(y\) | 2 | 1 | \(0\) |
| \(w\) | 2 | 2 | \(0\) |
| \(v\) | 2 | 3 | \(0\) |

## Computing the pages {#sec:spectral-pages}

Write $n=p+q$. For $r\geq1$, use the subspaces
$$
\begin{aligned}
Z_r^{p,n}&=\{c\in F^pC^n:dc\in F^{p+r}C^{n+1}\}\\
E_r^{p,q}&=\frac{Z_r^{p,n}}{Z_{r-1}^{p+1,n}+dZ_{r-1}^{p-r+1,n-1}}
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $Z_0^{p,n}=F^pC^n$. The map $d_r$ is induced by $d$ and has bidegree $(r,1-r)$. The diagrams below are generated by binary row reduction on this input. The basis table on each page gives the chosen lifts.

~~~spectral-sequence filtered-example
[
  {"id":"a","degree":0,"filtration":0,"d":["b"]},
  {"id":"x","degree":1,"filtration":0,"d":["y"]},
  {"id":"b","degree":1,"filtration":1,"d":[]},
  {"id":"u","degree":1,"filtration":1,"d":[]},
  {"id":"z","degree":1,"filtration":1,"d":["y","w"]},
  {"id":"y","degree":2,"filtration":1,"d":[]},
  {"id":"w","degree":2,"filtration":2,"d":[]},
  {"id":"v","degree":2,"filtration":3,"d":[]}
]
~~~

- On \(E_0\), the filtration-preserving part sends \(z\) to \(y\), so those classes vanish on \(E_1\).
- On \(E_1\), \(d_1[a]=[b]\). The class of \(x\) survives because \(y=d_0z\) on the associated graded.
- On \(E_2\), lift the surviving class by \(x+z\), since \(d(x+z)=w\). Therefore \(d_2[x+z]=[w]\).
- On \(E_3=E_\infty\), the survivors are \([u]\) in bidegree \((1,0)\) and \([v]\) in bidegree \((3,-1)\). Directly, \(H^1(C)\) is generated by the class of \(u\) and \(H^2(C)\) by the class of \(v\), with these filtration degrees.

The calculation applies to finite filtered complexes over \(\mathbb{F}_2\) given by an explicit filtered basis and differential. General coefficient rings, multiplicative extensions, and infinite filtrations require separate input and arguments.

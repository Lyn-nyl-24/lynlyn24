Remark 7.62#

Both the algebraic functor Spec(R)R) and the locally ringed space Prim(R)R) are universal objects with ring of functions RR in their respective categories:

(i) For any algebraic functor XX and ring map φ:R→𝒪(X),\varphi :R\rightarrow \mathcal{O}(X), there is a unique map X→X\rightarrow Spec(R)R) inducing φ\varphi on rings of functions.

(ii) For any locally ringed space XX and ring map φ:R→𝒪(X),\varphi :R\rightarrow \mathcal{O}(X), there is a unique map X→X\rightarrow Prim(R)R) inducing φ\varphi on rings of functions.

For Spec(R),R), this holds by “abstract nonsense” (Remark 2.54), whereas for Prim(R)R) it is the content of Theorem 7.59.

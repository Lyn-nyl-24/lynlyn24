Definition 7.44 · Ringed space#

Definition 7.48 · Locally ringed space#

Let kk be a ring. A locally kk-ringed space is a kk-ringed space (X,𝒪X)(X,\mathcal{O}_{X}) such that, for each x∈X,x\in X, the stalk 𝒪X,x\mathcal{O}_{X,x} is a local ring. If (X,𝒪X)(X,\mathcal{O}_{X}) and (Y,𝒪Y)(Y,\mathcal{O}_{Y}) are locally kk-ringed spaces, a morphism of locally kk-ringed spaces is a morphism of kk-ringed spaces such that, for each x∈X,x\in X, the map φx:𝒪Y,f(x)→𝒪X,x\varphi _{x}:\mathcal{O}_{Y,f(x)}\rightarrow \mathcal{O}_{X,x} is a local ring map. We denote by LRS the category of locally kk-ringed spaces, which is a (non-full!) subcategory of RS When k=ℤ,k=\mathbb{Z}, we write LRS = LRS for the category of locally ringed spaces.

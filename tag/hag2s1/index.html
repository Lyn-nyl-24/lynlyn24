Affine spaces

Section 2.1 · Affine geometry

Affine geometry studies the solutions in affine spaces to systems of polynomial equations, while projective geometry studies the solutions in projective spaces to systems of homogeneous polynomial equations. In both cases, the solutions form a functor CAlg Set from the category of kk-algebras to the category of sets. Such functors are the basic objects of algebraic geometry:

Definition 2.1 · Algebraic functor#

Let kk be a ring. An algebraic kk-functor is a functor CAlg Set. An algebraic ℤ\mathbb{Z}-functor is simply called an algebraic functor. Given an algebraic kk-functor XX and a kk-algebra R,R, the elements of X(R)X(R) are called the RR-valued points or RR-points of X.X.

A basic example is given by affine spaces:

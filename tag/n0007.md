## The unrolled exact couple {#sec:unrolled-exact-couple}

Let $C$ be a chain complex with an increasing filtration by subcomplexes $F_sC$. Put $A_s=H_*(F_sC)$ and $E_s=H_*(F_sC/F_{s-1}C)$. The short exact sequence for each filtration step induces

$$
\cdots\longrightarrow (A_{s-1})_n
\xrightarrow{\alpha_s}(A_s)_n
\xrightarrow{\beta_s}(E_s)_n
\xrightarrow{\gamma_s}(A_{s-1})_{n-1}\longrightarrow\cdots
$$

~~~figure unrolled-exact-couple
~~~

Solid straight arrows preserve total degree. The dashed diagonals are connecting maps of degree $-1$. For each $s$, the upper horizontal edge, the vertical edge, and the dashed diagonal form one exact triangle. The curved arrow records the composite $d_1=\beta_{s-1}\gamma_s$. Since $\gamma_{s-1}\beta_{s-1}=0$, the next composite vanishes and $d_1^2=0$.

The diagram uses the homological convention $E^1_{s,t}=(E_s)_{s+t}$, in which $d_1$ has bidegree $(-1,0)$. The [computed example](tag:n0006) uses cohomological grading and a decreasing filtration.

## Reference

The layout follows John Rognes, *Spectral Sequences*, §2.1, Definition 2.1.1 and Example 2.1.4. The curved differential is an addition to the unrolled layout.

Quasi-coherent modules on schemes

Section 7.6 · Schemes

If XX is an algebraic functor, the category Mod of quasi-coherent modules over XX is by definition a limit of a diagram of categories of modules over rings, with transition functors given by extension of scalars (Definition 4.13). For any ring R,R, the category Mod is an abelian category, and it is easy to show that the limit of a diagram of abelian categories and exact functors is again an abelian category. However, extension of scalars is only right exact and not exact in general, so that the category Mod is not necessarily abelian. If XX is a scheme, the situation is much better. Indeed, in the category of Zariski sheaves, we can write XX as the colimit of a diagram of affine schemes and open immersions. If j:j: Spec( Spec(A)A) is an open immersion of affine schemes, the ring map A→BA\rightarrow B is flat, so that the functor j∗:j^{\ast}: Mod Mod is exact.

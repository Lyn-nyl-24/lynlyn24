Pointless topology

Section 5.1 · Locales and topological spaces

The starting point of “pointless topology” is the observation that most topological spaces (including for example all Hausdorff spaces) are determined by their posets of open subsets. Since posets are ubiquitous in mathematics, this leads to the appearance of topology in unexpected places. In this chapter, we will see that the poset of open subfunctors of any algebraic functor XX is isomorphic to the poset of open subsets of an associated topological space ∣X∣,\vert X\vert , which we will describe explicitly when X=X= Spec(A)A) or X=X= Proj(A).A).

Notation 5.1#

Let PP be a poset. Given a family (xi)i∈I(x_{i})_{i\in I} in P,P, we denote by its supremum (least upper bound) and by its infimum (greatest lower bound), if they exist.

Remark 5.2#

(i) If we view a poset as a category, suprema are colimits and infima are limits.

(ii) The supremum of the empty family (i.e., the colimit of the empty diagram) is the smallest element (i.e., the initial object). Dually, the infimum of the empty family is the largest element.

(iii) A poset admits all suprema if and only if it admits all infima. Indeed, the infimum of a family is the supremum of all elements below the family.

Any totally ordered set with suprema is a locale. For example, the posets ℕ∪{+∞}\mathbb{N}\cup \{+\infty \} and ℝ∪{±∞}\mathbb{R}\cup \{\pm \infty \} are locales.

Proposition 5.10 · The adjunction between topological spaces and locales#

The functor Open: Top →\rightarrow Loc is left adjoint to the functor Pt: Loc →\rightarrow Top. This adjunction is idempotent, i.e., it restricts to an equivalence between the essential images of both functors.

Definition 5.11 · Sober spaces and spatial locales#

(i) A topological space TT is sober if it lies in the essential image of Pt: Loc →\rightarrow Top, or equivalently if the unit map T→T\rightarrow Pt(Open(T))T)) is an isomorphism.

(ii) A locale OO is spatial if it lies in the essential image of Open: Top →\rightarrow Loc, or equivalently if the counit map Open(Pt(O))→OO))\rightarrow O is an isomorphism.

Remark 5.12#

By Proposition 5.10, the adjunction Open ⊣\dashv Pt restricts to an equivalence between the category Top of sober topological spaces and the category Loc of spatial locales. Moreover, the inclusion Top Top has a left adjoint given by Pt ∘\circ Open, called soberification, and the inclusion Loc Loc has a right adjoint given by Open ∘\circ Pt, called spatialization. In particular, limits of sober spaces are sober and colimits of spatial locales are spatial.

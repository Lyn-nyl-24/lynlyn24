Weil divisors

Section 10.5 · Divisors and blowups

Weil divisors are an alternative approach to divisors that is in some sense much simpler, as it only depends on the underlying topological space of a scheme. Although Weil divisors are not well-behaved in general, we will show that they are equivalent to Cartier divisors on regular schemes, which is a vast generalization of Example 10.73(ii).

The unrolled exact couple #

Let CC be a chain complex with an increasing filtration by subcomplexes FsCF_sC. Put As=H∗(FsC)A_s=H_*(F_sC) and Es=H∗(FsC/Fs−1C)E_s=H_*(F_sC/F_{s-1}C). The short exact sequence for each filtration step induces

⋯⟶(As−1)n→αs(As)n→βs(Es)n→γs(As−1)n−1⟶⋯\cdots\longrightarrow (A_{s-1})_n \xrightarrow{\alpha_s}(A_s)_n \xrightarrow{\beta_s}(E_s)_n \xrightarrow{\gamma_s}(A_{s-1})_{n-1}\longrightarrow\cdots

Solid straight arrows preserve total degree. The dashed diagonals are connecting maps of degree −1-1. For each ss, the upper horizontal edge, the vertical edge, and the dashed diagonal form one exact triangle. The curved arrow records the composite d1=βs−1γsd_1=\beta_{s-1}\gamma_s. Since γs−1βs−1=0\gamma_{s-1}\beta_{s-1}=0, the next composite vanishes and d12=0d_1^2=0.

The diagram uses the homological convention Es,t1=(Es)s+tE^1_{s,t}=(E_s)_{s+t}, in which d1d_1 has bidegree (−1,0)(-1,0). The computed example uses cohomological grading and a decreasing filtration.

Reference

The layout follows John Rognes, Spectral Sequences, §2.1, Definition 2.1.1 and Example 2.1.4. The curved differential is an addition to the unrolled layout.

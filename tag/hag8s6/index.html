For completeness, let us mention the following module-theoretic characterization of regular rings, which is the key ingredient in the proof of Theorem 8.81(iii):

Theorem 8.84 · Serre’s homological characterization of regularity#

Let RR be a noetherian ring and let n∈ℕ∪{∞}.n\in \mathbb{N}\cup \{\infty \}\textit{.} The following conditions are equivalent:

(i) RR is regular of Krull dimension ≤n.\leq n\textit{.}

(ii) Every RR-module admits a finite projective resolution of length ≤n.\leq n\textit{.}

(iii) For every ideal I⊂R,I\subset R\textit{,} the RR-module R/IR/I admits a finite projective resolution of length ≤n.\leq n\textit{.}

Definition 3.69 · Quasi-projective scheme#

Let kk be a ring. An algebraic kk-functor XX is a quasi-projective kk-scheme if there exists an ℕ\mathbb{N}-graded kk-algebra A,A, generated by a finite subset of A1,A_{1}, and a finite homogeneous subset F⊂AF\subset A such that X≃X\simeq D(F)⊂F)\subset Proj(A).A). We denote by QProj Fun(CAlg Set) the full subcategory of quasi-projective kk-schemes.

Note that we could have assumed F⊂A1F\subset A_{1} without changing the definition: we can assume F⊂A+F\subset A_{+} by multiplying FF by a finite generating subset of A1,A_{1}, and if d≥1d\ge 1 is divisible by all degrees in F,F, we can then replace AA by A(d)A^{(d)} and each element of FF by its power in Ad.A_{d}. We will see in §3.9 that every quasi-affine kk-scheme of finite type is quasi-projective. Thus, under this finiteness assumption, quasi-projective kk-schemes subsume all types of kk-schemes discussed so far.

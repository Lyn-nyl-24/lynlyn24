We now investigate the behavior of Krull dimension under morphisms.

Definition 8.56 · Going-up and going-down properties#

Let f:Y→Xf:Y\rightarrow X be a map of topological spaces.

(i) ff has the going-up property if, for every y∈Yy\in Y and every specialization f(y)⇝x′,f(y)\rightsquigarrow x^{\prime}, there exists a specialization y⇝y′y\rightsquigarrow y^{\prime} with f(y′)=x′.f(y^{\prime})=x^{\prime}.

(ii) ff has the going-down property if, for every y∈Yy\in Y and every specialization x′⇝f(y),x^{\prime}\rightsquigarrow f(y), there exists a specialization y′⇝yy^{\prime}\rightsquigarrow y with f(y′)=x′.f(y^{\prime})=x^{\prime}. Note that any closed map has the going-up property, while any open map has the going-down property.

The following assertions follow immediately from the definitions:

Mathematical notation

Double-struck notation for number systems, projective space, and the sphere spectrum.

  • Number systems
  • Projective varieties
  • Sphere spectrum
  • References

Number systems #

The standard number systems are the natural numbers ℕ\mathbb{N}, the integers ℤ\mathbb{Z}, the rational numbers ℚ\mathbb{Q}, the real numbers ℝ\mathbb{R}, and the complex numbers ℂ\mathbb{C}. When relevant, specify whether ℕ\mathbb{N} includes zero. Write 𝔽q\mathbb{F}_q for the finite field of order qq, where qq is a prime power.

Projective varieties #

Write ℙkn\mathbb{P}^n_k for projective nn-space over the field kk. A projective variety XX may be presented by a closed immersion X↪ℙknX\hookrightarrow\mathbb{P}^n_k. The double-struck ℙ\mathbb{P} names the ambient projective space; the symbol XX denotes the chosen variety.

Sphere spectrum #

Write 𝕊=Σ∞S0\mathbb{S}=\Sigma^\infty S^0 for the sphere spectrum, where S0S^0 is the pointed zero-sphere. The notation SnS^n denotes a sphere as a space, while πn(𝕊)\pi_n(\mathbb{S}) is a stable homotopy group.

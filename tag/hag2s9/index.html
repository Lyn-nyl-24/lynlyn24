Finiteness properties

Section 2.9 · Affine geometry

Recall that a kk-algebra is of finite presentation if it is isomorphic to k[Σ]k[\Sigma ] where Σ is a system of finitely many polynomial equations in finitely many variables, and it is of finite type if it is isomorphic to k[Σ]k[\Sigma ] where Σ has finitely many variables (but any number of equations). We denote the respective full subcategories of CAlg by CAlg and CAlg It turns out that these finiteness conditions can naturally be expressed in terms of the algebraic kk-functor Spec(A):A): CAlg Set.

Definition 2.81 · Locally of finite presentation/type#

Let X:X: CAlg Set be an algebraic kk-functor.

(i) XX is locally of finite presentation if it preserves filtered colimits.

(ii) XX is locally of finite type if it preserves the colimits of filtered diagrams with injective transition maps.

Remark 2.84#

Since filtered colimits commute with finite limits in the category of sets, the condition of being locally of finite presentation or of finite type is preserved by finite limits in Fun(CAlg Set).

Zariski descent

Section 2.8 · Affine geometry

Let (fi)i∈I(f_{i})_{i\in I} be a family of element in a ring RR that generates the unit ideal. Then the intersection of the vanishing loci V(fi)f_{i}) is the empty scheme but, in general, it is not true that Spec(R)R) is the union of the nonvanishing loci D(fi)f_{i}) (see Example 2.61). In this section, we will show that this becomes true if we compute the union in the category of affine schemes. Concretely, this means that a map Spec(R)→R)\to Spec(S)S) is uniquely determined by a family of maps D(fi)→f_{i})\to Spec(S)S) that agree on all the intersections D(fi)∩f_{i})\cap D(fj).f_{j}). This is one of the most important results in the foundations of algebraic geometry, which we will later recast as the statement that affine schemes satisfy Zariski descent.

Definition 2.76 · Zariski-local property#

A property PP of modules (resp. of linear maps, of algebras, etc.) is Zariski-local if, for any ring RR and family (fi)i∈I(f_{i})_{i\in I} generating the unit ideal in R,R, an RR-module MM (resp. an RR-linear map M→N,M\to N, an RR-algebra A,A, etc.) has property PP if and only if, for each i∈I,i\in I, the RfiR_{fi}-module MfiM_{fi} (resp. the RfiR_{fi}-linear map Mfi→Nfi,M_{fi}\to N_{fi}, the RfiR_{fi}-algebra Afi,A_{fi}, etc.) has property P.P.

The following properties of modules are Zariski-local:

(i) being zero,

(ii) finite generation,

(iii) finite presentation,

(iv) projectivity,

(v) flatness.

The following properties of linear maps are Zariski-local:

(vi) being zero,

(vii) injectivity,

(viii) surjectivity,

(ix) bijectivity.

The following properties of algebras are Zariski-local:

(x) finite generation,

(xi) finite presentation,

(xii) finiteness,

(xiii) integrality.

The following properties of sequences of modules are Zariski-local:

(xiv) exactness.

Remark 2.78#

Further Zariski-local properties of modules are: being torsion, torsion-freeness. The following properties of modules are not Zariski-local: freeness, injectivity.

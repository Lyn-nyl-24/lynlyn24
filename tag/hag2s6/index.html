Remark 2.53 · Size issues#

The statement of Remark 2.52(iv) is actually nonsensical due to “size issues”. Since the category CAlg is large, the limit in the formula for 𝒪(X)\mathcal{O}(X) is indexed by a large category, so that the ring 𝒪(X)\mathcal{O}(X) is sometimes large. In this case, 𝒪(X)\mathcal{O}(X) is not an object of CAlg, which is the category of small rings. There are two standard ways to rectify this issue:

(i) One can simply replace the target of 𝒪\mathcal{O} by the category CAlg of large rings. One may then also

replace the category of sets in the source by the category Set of large sets. We obtain the functor

which is the unique extension of the embedding CAlg CAlg that preserves large limits.

(ii) One can replace the source of 𝒪\mathcal{O} by the subcategory Fun(CAlg Set) of accessible functors, which are the functors that are small colimits of representables. For an accessible functor X,X, 𝒪(X)\mathcal{O}(X) is a small limit of small rings and hence is small. We therefore have a functor

which is the unique extension of the identity CAlg →\to CAlg that preserves small limits. All algebraic functors that arise in practice (including all schemes) are accessible, and all relevant constructions preserve accessibility, so that this restriction does not have any undesirable consequences.

## Setup {#sec:kernel}

Let \(V\) and \(W\) be vector spaces over the same field and let \(T \colon V \to W\) be linear.

::: definition def:kernel | Kernel of a linear map
The **kernel** of \(T\) is the subspace
\[
\ker T = \{v \in V : T(v) = 0\} \label{eq:kernel}
\]
:::

## Fibres

::: proposition prop:kernel-cosets | Kernel cosets
For $v \in V$, the fibre $T^{-1}(T(v))$ is $v+\ker T$.
:::

::: proof pf:kernel-cosets | Kernel cosets
Linearity and \(\eqref{eq:kernel}\) give
$$
\begin{aligned}
T(u) = T(v) &\iff T(u-v)=0 \\
            &\iff u-v\in\ker T
\end{aligned}
$$
The last condition is equivalent to $u\in v+\ker T$. The fibres form a partition as in [the equivalence-relation proposition](ref:prop:partition).
:::

## Factorisation through the quotient

::: theorem thm:linear-factorization | First isomorphism theorem
The assignment $[v]\mapsto T(v)$ induces a linear isomorphism $V/\ker T \to \image T$.
:::

::: proof pf:linear-factorization | First isomorphism theorem
[The kernel-coset proposition](ref:prop:kernel-cosets) proves well-definedness and injectivity. Linearity descends from $T$, and surjectivity follows from the definition of $\image T$.
:::

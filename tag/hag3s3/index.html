Remark 3.26#

(i) Unlike the affine space construction the constructions and are not contravariantly functorial on the whole category of sets, but only on the subcategory of sets and surjections.

(ii) A technical difference between affine and projective spaces is that 𝔸I\mathbb{A}^{I} is quasi-compact for all II (this property will be defined in §7.3), whereas ℙI\mathbb{P}^{I} and 𝔸I−0\mathbb{A}^{I}- 0 are quasi-compact if and only if II is finite. For this reason, many basic results in projective geometry assume a finite number of variables.
